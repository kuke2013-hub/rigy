# Mandatory VRPod/Rigy anatomical invariant
Every future change must preserve the medical-reference-based joint rotation limits regardless of animation, IK, saved poses or controllers. Knees and elbows are anatomical hinges: sideways joint point movement comes through valid hip/shoulder rotations, never off-axis hinge twists. Preserve the pelvis centre, four hand/foot anchors, all 12 grab points, Zelda v13 controls, two-grip scaling, and original FBX. Test real bone rotations and continuity across several frames, collisions, anchors, both lateral directions, and hardware if claiming Quest success. Do not confuse conservative engineering proxy limits with clinical normal limits. Save independent version commits and rollback branches. Do not declare success solely because joint point moved.
